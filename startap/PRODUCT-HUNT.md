## Description (≤ 500 белги)

> Ekomun is a free ecology learning platform for Uzbekistan, in Uzbek (Cyrillic & Latin), Russian and English. Pupils and students learn through 20+ courses, 400+ interactive lessons and quizzes, 137 curated video lessons, 3D virtual labs and a chemistry lab room with 196 reagents. It covers environmental law, recycling and climate, gives QR-verifiable certificates and works offline as an Android app. Teachers turn their own PDFs into game-based lessons.

## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt! 👋 I'm Shukurjon, an ecology educator from Uzbekistan.
>
> In 2025 our government set a goal to build ecological culture in 4.2 million school pupils by 2030 — but there was no interactive ecology platform in Uzbek. Textbooks were dry, YouTube was scattered, and many schools have weak internet.
>
> So we built **Ekomun**:
> 🎮 lessons as games, crosswords, Venn diagrams and cinquains (based on the official textbooks)
> 🧪 3D labs and a virtual lab room — do experiments a rural school can't afford
> ⚖️ environmental law explained for kids and students
> 📱 an Android app that works fully offline
> 🎓 QR-verifiable certificates and a teacher workshop (PDF → interactive lesson)
>
> It's free for learners. Schools and universities can get a licence with class reports.
>
> I'd love your feedback: **what would make you recommend an ecology app to a teacher you know?** 🌱
